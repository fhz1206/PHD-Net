test(P158): check P154 against the Ascend probe results and add two verifiers

fhz's Ascend 910B4 probe with torch 2.9.0+cpu and torch_npu 2.9.0 changes one
instruction materially. Both float8_e4m3fn and float8_e5m2 fail to even be
created on the NPU with "has not been supported", for create, roundtrip and
matmul alike, while on the CPU they can be created and round-tripped but cannot be
matmul-ed. Since fp8 tensors cannot exist on the device, the second half of the
instruction, fp8 storage, is physically impossible there. The real 910B path is
int8 storage with int8 compute, which costs exactly the same traffic since both
are one byte per element. Whether to accept that form needs fhz's decision.

That result also makes the P154 CPU bit conversion dead code on Ascend, because
its guard requires the weight dtype to be float8. So fp8_to_int8_cpu is never
called, which is good news since the D2H and H2D synchronisation I was worried
about cannot happen. What actually needs measuring on Ascend is the P155 int16
intermediate with int32 accumulation. I verified that path locally with the new
tools/verify_int8_fallback.py, covering the forward shape, the update, and the
auto switch; int8 storage with int8 compute gives nll 10.7530.

One caveat about the probe itself: every npu_quant_* entry failed on argument
signatures rather than on support, for instance "missing value for argument
'scale'" and "takes 1 positional argument(s) but 2 was/were given". Those results
do not show that Ascend lacks int8 operators, so they must be retested with full
signatures before drawing that conclusion. npu_quant_matmul_reduce_sum is the
most promising candidate since it reports only that x2 must be in nz format, and
npu_format_cast works, so the layout may be reachable; its semantics of computing
quantised group matmuls and summing all groups could match our int32 accumulation
directly.

That means the test order I gave earlier was wrong. Step T3 assumed int8
operators were already running and asked for msprof cube_time, but on this device
nothing int8 executes, so that measurement would be meaningless. The new first step
is to probe the npu_quant family with correct signatures and only run msprof if a
real int8 operator turns up.
