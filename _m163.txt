refactor(P163): lock fp16, disable the int family outright, and drop one-off probes

fhz asked to keep the version that was just verified, to remove the extra scripts,
to disable every int path, and to delete the log and bug-hunting files.

The evidence is the production run: on the 910B the chain fp8 to int8 to fp16
failed at fp8 with ERR01007 and then failed at int8 as well, so it landed on fp16.
That makes fp16 the default and it is exactly the configuration that was verified,
with the readout on the accelerator at 10.15 ms per token, 4.853 ms of that in the
readout at 48.1 percent of the step and no OOV skips.

In precision_policy there is now a single INT_FAMILY_ENABLED switch set to False,
and the default model dtype is fp16. candidate_order raises ValueError when an int
dtype is requested rather than silently substituting something else, and the fp
chain drops the int family along with fp8 and fp4, giving fp16 to bf16, bf16 to
fp16, fp32 to fp32 and fp8 to fp16 to bf16, with fp32 no longer a forced fallback.
The FAMILIES entries themselves stay so history remains readable and restoring
support on another platform is a single flip. In train.py the readout dtype
default becomes fp16 and the choices narrow to fp32, fp16 and bf16.

Five one-off probes are gone: probe_fp8, probe_npu_quant, diag_readout_npu,
diag_torchnpu and verify_int8_fallback, plus the earlier verify_p154_ascend.
Kept are probe_readout_precision because the P110 retention evidence matters and
the CLI help cites it, and audit_precision plus bench_accel because CI runs them.
Nothing was removed from tests/verifiers since those are the acceptance gates;
instead the int cases now assert that an int request must be rejected, which turns
the decision itself into part of the contract. That gate passes 35 out of 35 and
the rest are all PASS.

Two lessons recorded. First, leaving entries in the degradation chain that are
known to fail buries the real cause, which is what happened before the _dev bug
was found, so a disabled path should be removed rather than left as decoration.
Second, a gate must not be deleted when a feature is turned off; it should be
rewritten to assert that the off state holds, since deleting it loses regression
protection while rewriting it locks the decision into the gate.

Gates: fast 9/9, precision_policy 35/35, accel_readout, probe_device,
accel_sparse, cann_env, help_percent, ms_stream, powlaw_readout, readout_intdtypes
and ckpt_roundtrip all PASS, --help exit 0.
